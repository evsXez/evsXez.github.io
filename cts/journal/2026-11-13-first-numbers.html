First numbers, and why this journal

13 November 2026

Two and a half weeks in, from the funnel in CTS:

88applications sent
56confirmed by the company
16replies
1 + 2screening calls held + invitations

The pace: 2 applications a day in the first week, then from 9 November between 8 and 28 a day. My target is 50 a day, and I have not hit it once. It stays as a target.

The pool behind them: about 25,800 postings read, 641 companies watched, around 21,000 filtered out by rules before I ever saw them.

In my first screening call, the recruiter had already opened the CTS page and recognised the project. That is why this journal exists. A system you built to find a job says something about how you work: what you automate, what you refuse to automate, and how you check that it is right.

My rough expectation, from what the market looks like now, is an offer somewhere between 300 and 600 applications. Maybe more. So this is a long game, and the system matters more than any single application.

From now on I will write here every few days, with a short post on LinkedIn for each entry. The plan for the next entries: the first results of the CV experiment, how the scoring changed after I labelled the openings it got wrong, and what the interviews teach.
