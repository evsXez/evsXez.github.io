An A/B test on my own CV

8 November 2026 · written on 13 November 2026, looking back

Everyone says you should tailor your CV to each posting. Nobody I read had numbers. So since 8 November it is an experiment.

When I press "Prepare" on a job card, CTS flips a coin. Group A gets my standard CV for that kind of role. Group B gets a CV adapted to the wording of the posting: the role name in the first sentence of the summary, the posting's terms in the bullets and skills where I have real experience behind them.

Guard rails, enforced in code:

  • a term from the posting is used only if my approved experience backs it;
  • no more than a few words in a row copied from the posting;
  • the title may change only the generic word (Mobile, Software, Product), never the level;
  • group B never falls back to the standard CV, otherwise the comparison breaks.

The metric is simple: replies and screens per application in each group. It needs more applications before it says anything. I will post the result here either way.
